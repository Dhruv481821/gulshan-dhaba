GENERATIONS OF TASTE

More than a stop on the highway.

Gulshan Dhaba has been part of the Murthal food journey since 1950. Today, the same spirit lives on through classic North Indian recipes, signature parathas and the simple pleasure of eating well on the road.

MURTHAL · SINCE 1950

OUR STORY

From a highway stop to a Murthal institution.

What began as a traditional dhaba became a familiar destination for generations of travelers. The identity is still rooted in the same essentials: hot food, traditional preparation, generous portions and a welcoming place to take a break from the road.

Today, Gulshan continues that legacy with vegetarian North Indian favourites, tandoor-cooked breads and the unmistakable combination of paratha and white butter.
